## What's covered

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="National IDs" icon="id-card">
    US Social Security Numbers (validated against real SSA numbering rules, not just the `###-##-####` shape), India Aadhaar numbers (validated with the Verhoeff checksum), and India PAN numbers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Financial identifiers" icon="building-columns">
    India GSTIN and IFSC codes, and bank account numbers. Severity varies by subtype — a bank code format is generally lower risk than what looks like a complete, real account number.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Example (masked)

```text
Detected: us_ssn
Masked value shown to you: ***-**-5190
Severity: high → BLOCK
```

## Where this applies

- Chat messages — via [scan_prompt](/how-scanning-works/scanning-messages)
- Text-based uploaded files and forms — via [scan_document](/how-scanning-works/scanning-files)

When you share a form or ID card as text, your assistant is instructed to extract every field into the scan, not just the parts that obviously look sensitive — see [Scanning Messages](/how-scanning-works/scanning-messages).
